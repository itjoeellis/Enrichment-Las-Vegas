More About Enrichment

Enrichment Las Vegas brings me back to my childhood at Canisius College. They would hold a program in the summer and only a few kids would get in, I was fortunate enough to attend for several years. We would play a variety of sports throughout the day and we would have that one period of enrichment, where the college athletes would teach us about different things, such as; healthy living, making better choices, what to expect in college as an athlete, peer pressure, and self esteem.

Enrichment Las Vegas takes my experiences as a child, teenager, high school and college student athlete, mental health professional, and father to create an atmosphere where kids can thrive, learn from each others mistakes, hold each other accountable, develop new confidence and discover the benefits of positive self talk.

College Athlete

At SUU , 2004 to 2006: I played corner back, accumulated 60+ tackles, had nine pass break ups (PBU), two tackles for loss (TFL), two fumble recoveries (FR) and three interception (INT). At Phoenix Community College: I played corner, receiver and returned kicks and was a pre-season Player to Watch as well as an all-WSFL performer and made all conference. Tallied 60 tackles with three interceptions (INT) and 10 pass break-ups (PBU) in my two seasons at PCC. At Turner Carroll HS in Buffalo, N.Y.: I played quarterback, passing for over 3,500 yards and rushing for over 1,750 during his career. Earned all-state and all-Catholic High School honors while helping the Chargers win multiple division championships. Lettered in basketball and winning two New York State Championships. Trained and developed under Dr. Matt Rhea's at Southern Utah University and "House" (Joe Kenn) at Arizona State University.

Counseling Experience

Counseling Experience I've been in the Behavioral and Mental Health field for over 17 years. I started working in group homes while attending college, eventually managing over 33 homes. My experience varies from ODD (oppositional defiant disorder), schizophrenia, conduct disorder, bipolar, autistic, separation anxiety disorder, and handicapped. My passion has always been to help kids, adolescents, and adults obtain their full potential and live a healthy and happy life

Outside the Box (OTB)

Outside the Box (OTB) is a nationally recognized 7v7 football team, known as OTB Elite. The program offers superb training for youth in the Phoenix, AZ area. OTB has personally helped dozens of high school athletes reach their dream to play at a college level.

Coaching

Coaching is a passion of mine, it is the reason I have dedicated the last seven years as a volunteer coach, while still holding a full time job, and being an active father. My coaching experience include: -The OTB program for 7 years. -Manager at Mountain Ridge Little League for 5 years. -Coach with Vegas Flag for 2 years. -Manager for Battle Born baseball travel team for 2 years. I personally know how important it is to have good coaches in our lives. The coaches I've had in my life, have helped me build character, confidence, sense of humor, discipline, and my attention to detail. My goal as a coach has always been to give the kids one of their best experiences; while, developing in multiple life skills, as well as making lifetime friendships.
