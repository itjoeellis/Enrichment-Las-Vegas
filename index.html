Welcome to Enrichment Las Vegas

Enrichment Las Vegas was founded on an idea that implementing solutions to real life situations through exercise, conditioning, agility, plyometrics, and mobility allows every child the chance to apply these solutions to boost confidence and self-esteem. Good sportsmanship at Enrichment Las Vegas promotes healthy living, awareness of the effects of peer pressure, being held accountable, overcoming difficult situations, all while remaining disciplined and humble.

CURRICULUM

When the children adapt to this curriculum and practice the skills taught and implemented their life will change. This curriculum is unique and you will not find it anywhere else. It’s a mentality!!!

LEGACY

Legacy has a lot to do with family, teachers, coaches, friends and life experiences. Our goal is to pass this vital information to the youth, so eventually they can pass these tools to their family, friends, and eventually the next generations.

GROWTH

Growth is endless, we believe a child can help push a family for the better and once we’re receptive to new energy and ideas the growth becomes constant. We are committed to helping children be successful in many aspects of their life.


ENVIRONMENT

We do our best to create a positive environment for the children, parents, and all parties involved with Enrichment Las Vegas. We encourage parents/guardians to step out their comfort zone, meet new people, get active, and put away the phone. Less distractions brings positive attractions!!!

VALUES

Enrichment Las Vegas has been built on these core values: effective communication, honesty, commitment, passion, reliability, loyalty, consistency, integrity, responsibility, and positivity. These value are implemented and practiced on a daily basis.

CULTURE

We welcome, serve, value, and respect our families and children from many different walks of life, to help us enrich Enrichment Las Vegas.
